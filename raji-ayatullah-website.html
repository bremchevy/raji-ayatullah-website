The evaluation rubric, opened up

The personal framework behind the project card above, combined with the criteria used daily on Outlier AI, Scale AI, Appen, OneForma and Test IO work. Select a criterion to see exactly what gets checked — and what gets a response flagged.

Factual accuracy

factual-accuracy.mdCore framework · Outlier AI

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • Every factual claim is verified — independent research and cross-referencing across technical and general-knowledge domains to fact-check the content.
  • Factual errors are identified and separated from opinion, inference and genuinely uncertain claims.
  • Claims made without support are called out as unsupported, even when they sound plausible.
Flagged when: hallucinations, misinformation or unsupported claims appear in the response.

Instruction-following

instruction-following.mdCore framework · Outlier AI

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • Adherence to the instructions in the prompt — the response is judged against what was actually asked, not what it chose to answer.
  • Compliance with project-specific guidelines and evaluation rubrics, including format, scope and constraint requirements.
  • Model behaviour is also tested under varied instruction sets, with response patterns documented for iterative refinement.
Flagged when: requirements are missed, constraints are ignored, or the response drifts off-instruction.

Logical consistency

logical-consistency.mdCore framework · Freelance evaluation

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • The response is read end-to-end for internal consistency — statements must not contradict each other.
  • Logical gaps are identified systematically — conclusions that do not follow from the steps before them.
  • Overall coherence is assessed against defined benchmarks, across science, technology, creative writing and reasoning tasks.
Flagged when: inconsistencies, contradictions or reasoning failures are found.

Communication quality

communication-quality.mdCore framework · Outlier AI

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • Clarity — whether the answer can be followed without re-reading, and whether the structure helps or hinders.
  • Grammar and written accuracy, held to the same editorial standard as proofreading and editing work.
  • Tone — appropriate for the task and consistent from start to finish.
Flagged when: tonal inconsistencies, unclear writing or grammar issues undermine the answer.

Reasoning quality

reasoning-quality.mdOutlier AI · AI Data Contributor

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • The quality of the reasoning itself is judged, not just the final answer — each step is assessed on its own merit.
  • Reasoning is tested across diverse domains — science, technology, creative writing and dedicated reasoning tasks.
  • In side-by-side comparisons, the response with the stronger reasoning is selected under the established criteria.
Flagged when: reasoning failures — broken chains, skipped steps or conclusions the working does not support.

Relevance

relevance.mdOutlier AI · Scale AI · Appen

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • Relevance of the content to the request — AI-generated content is reviewed for relevance alongside accuracy and clarity.
  • Usefulness — the depth and usefulness of the generated output is assessed, not just its surface correctness.
  • Fit for the audience and task the content was produced for.
Flagged when: the response is off-topic, padded, or correct but not actually useful for the request.

Completeness

completeness.mdOutlier AI · AI Data Contributor

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • Completeness is scored as its own criterion — every part of the request must be covered, not just the first part.
  • Multi-part prompts are broken down and checked part by part against the response.
  • Depth is weighed alongside coverage — a complete answer that stays shallow is marked down on usefulness.
Flagged when: parts of the request are left unanswered or the response stops short.

Safety

safety.mdOutlier AI · Scale AI · Appen

WHAT IS CHECKED

  • Safety is reviewed as a standing criterion on every response — one of the three headline checks in the professional summary, alongside accuracy and instruction adherence.
  • Content is assessed against the project-specific guidelines and safety requirements for that engagement.
  • Safety and compliance discipline from administrative work — including confidential information handling — carries into evaluation work.
Flagged when: content breaches the safety guidelines for the project, whatever its other scores.
How a verdict is reached: Score against the rubric→ Compare side-by-side & rank→ Justify in writing
